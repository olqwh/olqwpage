# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Current students of Our Lady Queen of the World Catholic Academy (Richmond Hill, Ontario). They mostly visit on phones, between classes or on the way to school, looking for a resource, a club, or a date.

## Product Purpose
olqw.ca is an unofficial, student-made hub. It points students to student resources, clubs and councils, the school-year calendar, and the official school site (olqwh.ycdsb.ca). Success means a student finds what they came for in one or two taps, and nobody mistakes the site for an official school or board page.

## Brand Personality
Warm, bright, friendly. It reads as made by students for students: plain-spoken, upbeat, never corporate. The motto "Choose love always" is the emotional anchor.

## Anti-references
- The official YCDSB or school websites. This site must never be mistaken for an official page, so it uses no crests, logos, or board styling.
- Generic SaaS or startup landing-page templates: hero metrics, icon card grids, gradient text.
- No logos, symbols, icons, or pictures of any kind (a hard requirement from the owner).

## Design Principles
1. Unofficial, out loud. A notice bar at the very top of every page states the unaffiliation, and the full disclaimer sits at the bottom.
2. Typography carries the identity. With no imagery, scale, weight, and color do the work.
3. One tap to anywhere. The home page is a short list of large targets.
4. Placeholders stay honest. Empty pages say plainly that content is coming instead of inventing facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at 4.5:1 or better, visible focus, keyboard reachable, 44px tap targets, skip link, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
